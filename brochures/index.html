Brochures

Brochures & downloadable overviews.

One-page-feeling overviews of each AmbiSecure platform, designed to be skimmed, shared, and printed. Each is a stand-alone HTML page — print to PDF from your browser if you need an offline copy.

No registration wall, no lead-capture form. Everything that’s on the rest of the site is on the brochures — in a denser format.

What these are, and which one to open

Each brochure is a condensed technical overview of one AmbiSecure platform: what it is, the hardware and protocols behind it, how it deploys, and what an integration actually involves. They are written for the engineer or architect doing the evaluation, not as marketing collateral — expect chip families, protocol names, and lifecycle detail rather than benefit bullets.

The six below split along two lines. Pick by the problem you are solving:

  • Authentication and workforce identity — start with OnePass platform for the full picture, or FIDO deployment if you already know you need phishing-resistant login and want the AAGUID, attestation, and recovery detail.
  • Credential issuance at scale — PKI & credential lifecycle covers X.509 issuance, registration authority design, key custody, and audit for workforce and government programmes.
  • Cards and applets — JavaCard platform covers multi-applet card architecture where several credentials share one chip.
  • Transit — Transit security covers DESFire EV2/EV3 with SAM-backed offline trust for closed-loop ticketing.
  • Connected devices — Device identity / IoT covers putting a secure element on the device BOM, personalising it on the SMT line, and rotating credentials in the field.

Every brochure is a normal HTML page, so it is searchable, linkable, and prints cleanly to PDF from the browser. If you need the underlying reasoning rather than the summary, each one links through to the relevant solution pages and engineering blog posts.
